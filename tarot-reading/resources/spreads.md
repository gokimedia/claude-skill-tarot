# Common Tarot Spreads

Source: https://deckaura.com/pages/tarot-spreads

## Three-Card Spread
Positions: Past · Present · Future
Use for: quick readings, daily guidance, understanding a situation's arc

## Celtic Cross (10-card)
1. Present situation
2. Challenge/cross
3. Past foundation
4. Recent past
5. Possible outcome (above)
6. Immediate future
7. You (the querent)
8. Environment/others
9. Hopes and fears
10. Final outcome

## Relationship Spread
1. You
2. Them
3. The connection
4. Strengths
5. Challenges
6. Action to take

## Year Ahead Spread (12 cards)
One card for each month of the coming year + one "theme of the year" central card.

## Decision Spread
1. The situation
2. Option A
3. Option B
4. What to consider
5. Best choice

## Yes/No Reading
Single card. Use the card's yes/no field. Upright yes-cards = affirmative, reversed = hesitant or conditional.
